## Slide 2

DIGITAL TWINS AS SOCIO-TECHNICAL SYSTEMS AND IT/OT/ET

CONVERGENCE

THE DIGITAL TWIN CONSORTIUM (DTC) DEFINES DIGITAL TWIN AS “A

VIRTUAL REPRESENTATION OF REAL-WORLD ENTITIES AND PROCESSES

SYNCHRONIZED AT A SPECIFIED FREQUENCY AND FIDELITY , USING REAL-

TIME AND HISTORICAL DATA TO REPRESENT THE PAST AND PRESENT AND

TO SIMULATE PREDICTED FUTURES”.

IN THIS FRAMING, A DIGITAL TWIN IS NOT SIMPLY A MODEL OR

DASHBOARD; IT IS A SOCIO-TECHNICAL SYSTEM WHOSE VALUE EMERGES

FROM HOW IT SUPPORTS DECISIONS AND INTERVENTIONS IN REAL-LIFE

OPERATIONS.

## Slide 3

DTC’S EMPHASIS ON IT/OT/ET CONVERGENCE PROVIDES A USEFUL

EDUCATIONAL LENS:

 INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY→IT (DATA COLLECTION, PROCESSING,

STORAGE, COMMUNICATION);

 OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY→OT (MONITORING/CONTROL OF

ESSENTIALLY PHYSICAL PROCESSES); AND

 ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY→ET (MODELING AND SYSTEM DESIGN).

MUST BE INTEGRATED INTO COHERENT INTERVENTION.

## Slide 11

CAPABILITY LOGIC→WHAT / AI AGENT LOGIC→HOW HOW AI AGENT SECOND, STUDENTS DECIDE SELECTED FUNCTIONS WILL BE PERFORMED USING THE

CAPABILITIES PERIODIC TABLE CATEGORIES IN THE SAME FORMAT: PW (PERCEPTION & KNOWLEDGE), CG (COGNITION & REASONING), LA (LEARNING & ADAPTATION), AE (ACTION & EXECUTION), IC (INTERACTION & COLLABORATION), AND GS (GOVERNANCE & SAFETY).

THIS SEPARATION IS ESPECIALLY IMPORTANT IN EDUCATIONAL SETTINGS WHERE GENERATIVE AI CAN BIAS TEAMS TOWARD SOLUTIONS THAT SOUND POWERFUL BUT HAVE UNCLEAR REQUIREMENTS, WEAK GOVERNANCE, OR POOR EXECUTION REALISM.

IT IS WORTHWHILE TO EMPHASIZE THE ROLE OF THE SYSTEM PERFORMANCE MATRIX (EXCELLENCE LEVEL AGAINST READINESS DEGREES), IN THE CONTEXT OF DTC’S AI AGENT CAPABILITIES PERIDIC TABLE, IN TERMS OF 11 CAPABILITIES OF ORGANIZATIONAL INTELLIGENCE AGAINST 12 CAPABILITIES IC-INTERACTION & COLLABORATIONS.

TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE DEGREE OF EMBEDDED TECHNOLOGY, IN ADDITION TO STATIC AUTOMATION (TYPE 0), DTC’S AIACPT CONSIDERS 4 TYPES OF MORE SOPHISTICATED AI AGENTS:

0- STATIC AUTOMATION: PRE-PROGRAMMED RESPONSES WITH NO LEARNING OR ADAPTATION & TRADITIONAL RULE-BASED SYMBOLIC AI SYSTEMS AND BASIC AUTOMATION.

1- CONVERSATIONAL AGENTS: NATURAL LANGUAGE INTERACTION WITH BASIC CONTEXT MANAGEMENT & CUSTOMER SERVICE CHATBOTS, FAQ SYSTEMS, VOICE ASSISTANT.

2-PROCEDURAL WORKFLOW AGENTS: MULTI-STEP TASK EXECUTION WITH TOOL INTEGRATION AND ROLE-BASED COLLABORATION & WORKFLOW AUTOMATION, TASK ORCHESTRATION, PROCESS MANAGEMENT.

3-COGNITIVE AUTONOMOUS AGENTS: SELF-DIRECTED PLANNING, LEARNING FROM EXPERIENCE, SOPHISTICATED REASONING & RESEARCH ASSISTANTS, PREDICTIVE SYSTEMS, AUTONOMOUS DECISION-MAKING.

CONSOLIDATION OF THE TWO-LAYER LOGIC USED IN THE INSTRUCTIONAL PATTERN.

WHAT AT THE (TWIN) LAYER, TEAMS MAKE A REQUIREMENTS-ORIENTED SELECTION OF DIGITAL TWIN CAPABILITY CATEGORIES (DS/IR/IC/UX/MG/TW) TO JUSTIFY WHAT THE DSQ MUST PROVIDE TO SUPPORT DECISIONS.

HOW AT THE (TWIN/AGENT) LAYER, TEAMS MAKE A REQUIREMENTS-ORIENTED SELECTION OF AI-AGENT CAPABILITY CATEGORIES (PW/CG/LA/AE/IC/GS) TO JUSTIFY HOW SELECTED DSQ FUNCTIONS WILL BE IMPLEMENTED TO SUPPORT DECISIONS, WITH THE CHOICE OF AGENT MODALITY (CONVERSATIONAL, WORKFLOW, AUTONOMOUS, OR COLLABORATIVE) EXPLICITLY CONSTRAINED BY GOVERNANCE AND SAFETY REQUIREMENTS.

## Slide 18

IT/OT/ET CONVERGENCE AND WHY IT IS CENTRAL TO “DIGITAL TWIN-FIRST”

DTs BECOME MOST CHALLENGING AND MOST VALUABLE PRECISELY WHEN THEY SIT AT THE INTERSECTION OF IT (INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

SYSTEMS AND DATA/ANALYTICS), OT (OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY SUCH AS SENSORS, AUTOMATION, AND INDUSTRIAL CONTROL), AND ET

(ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY SUCH AS MODELS, DESIGN INTENT, AND SYSTEM ENGINEERING ARTIFACTS).

IN PRACTICE, DT MUST RECONCILE HETEROGENEOUS DATA SOURCES AND TIME SCALES:

 IT OFTEN OPERATES ON BUSINESS RHYTHMS AND TRANSACTIONAL CORRECTNESS;

 OT OPERATES ON REAL-TIME CONSTRAINTS, SAFETY, AND RELIABILITY; AND

ET OPERATES ON DESIGN FIDELITY, PHYSICS, AND LIFECYCLE REASONING.

WHEN THESE WORLDS CONVERGE, REQUIREMENTS ALSO CONVERGE:

 INTEGRATION AND DATA SERVICES CANNOT BE SEPARATED FROM TRUSTWORTHINESS, AND INTELLIGENCE CANNOT BE

SEPARATED FROM GOVERNANCE AND OPERATIONAL SAFETY; AND

 THE INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN LEVERAGES THIS CONVERGENCE TO MAKE STUDENTS PRACTICE REALISTIC REASONING.

REAL-LIFE SYSTEM-IN-QUESTION (RSQ) AND THE CORRESPONDING DIGITAL SYSTEM-IN-QUESTION (DSQ) BOUNDARIES MUST EXPLICITLY INCLUDE WHERE IT ENDS AND OT BEGINS (E.G., WHAT DATA IS AVAILABLE, AT WHAT CADENCE, UNDER WHAT SECURITY CONSTRAINTS) AND HOW ET REPRESENTATIONS ARE MAINTAINED (E.G., WHETHER THE DSQ INCLUDES ENGINEERING MODELS, ASSUMPTIONS, AND THEIR VERSIONING).

## Slide 19

FROM DIGITAL REPLICA TO DECISION REFERENCE

A DT BECOMES STRATEGICALLY MEANINGFUL WHEN IT HELPS DECISION-MAKERS CHOOSE AMONG ALTERNATIVES AND JUSTIFY CHANGE SEQUENCING.

WHEN STUDENTS TREAT DT AS A REPLICA, THEIR WORK TENDS TO OPTIMIZE FIDELITY AGAINST TODAY’S CONDITIONS.

WHEN THEY TREAT DT AS A REFERENCE, THEIR WORK TENDS TO OPTIMIZE DECISION DEFENSIBILITY TOWARD A FUTURE TARGET.

THIS MATTERS BECAUSE REAL SYSTEMS ARE NOT STATIC: CONSTRAINTS CHANGE, OPERATIONS EVOLVE, RISKS MATERIALIZE, AND THE INFORMATION ENVIRONMENT SHIFTS.

TREATING THE CORRESPONDING DT AS A REFERENCE FORCES STUDENTS TO ARTICULATE A DESIRED FUTURE STATE AND MAKE THE PATHWAY EXPLICIT, RATHER THAN IMPLICITLY ASSUMING THAT IMPROVEMENT IS THE NATURAL CONSEQUENCE OF MEASUREMENT.
