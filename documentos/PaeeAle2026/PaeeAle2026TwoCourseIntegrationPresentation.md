## Slide 2

DIGITAL TWINS AS SOCIO-TECHNICAL SYSTEMS AND IT/OT/ET

CONVERGENCE

THE DIGITAL TWIN CONSORTIUM (DTC) DEFINES DIGITAL TWIN AS “A

VIRTUAL REPRESENTATION OF REAL-WORLD ENTITIES AND PROCESSES

SYNCHRONIZED AT A SPECIFIED FREQUENCY AND FIDELITY , USING REAL-

TIME AND HISTORICAL DATA TO REPRESENT THE PAST AND PRESENT AND

TO SIMULATE PREDICTED FUTURES”.

IN THIS FRAMING, A DIGITAL TWIN IS NOT SIMPLY A MODEL OR

DASHBOARD; IT IS A SOCIO-TECHNICAL SYSTEM WHOSE VALUE EMERGES

FROM HOW IT SUPPORTS DECISIONS AND INTERVENTIONS IN REAL-LIFE

OPERATIONS.

## Slide 3

DTC’S EMPHASIS ON IT/OT/ET CONVERGENCE PROVIDES A USEFUL

EDUCATIONAL LENS:

 INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY→IT (DATA COLLECTION, PROCESSING,

STORAGE, COMMUNICATION);

 OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY→OT (MONITORING/CONTROL OF

ESSENTIALLY PHYSICAL PROCESSES); AND

 ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY→ET (MODELING AND SYSTEM DESIGN).

MUST BE INTEGRATED INTO COHERENT INTERVENTION.

PEDAGOGICALLY , THIS PUSHES INTERDISCIPLINARY COLLABORATION: COMPUTING STUDENTS CANNOT TREAT PRODUCTION SYSTEMS AS A SIMPLE “DOMAIN BLACK BOX”, AND PRODUCTION ENGINEERING STUDENTS CANNOT CONSIDER DIGITALIZATION AS AN EXTERNAL LAYER THAT APPEARS AFTER PROCESS MAPPING.
